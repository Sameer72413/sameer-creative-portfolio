UrbanVibe.

Wear Your Vibe.

A college-fashion advertising concept built around self-expression, everyday comfort and personal style.

Project type: Independent creative practice

01. The Project Brief

Develop an advertising concept for a student-focused T-shirt brand that makes everyday fashion feel personal.

02. Target Audience

College students who enjoy casual fashion and want their clothing to reflect their personality and individual style.

03. The Challenge

Instead of focusing only on the T-shirt, the campaign should connect the product with something meaningful to its audience.

04. Human Insight

People often use clothing to express who they are without needing to explain themselves in words.

05. The Big Idea

Wear Your Vibe.

Your style is more than what you wear. It is a way to express your personality, mood and individuality.

06. Headline Options

Option 1 — Main headline

Wear Your Vibe.

Option 2

Your Style. Your Story.

Option 3

Be Yourself. Wear It Your Way.

07. Sample Advertisement Copy

Different moods. Different personalities.

One style that feels like you.

UrbanVibe — Wear Your Vibe.

08. Short Video Ad Concept

Format: 15–20 second social-media advertisement

Scene 1 — Different styles

College students arrive wearing different casual outfits.

Scene 2 — Express yourself

Each student shows their personality through their style.

Scene 3 — Together

The students come together, celebrating their individuality.

Closing screen

UrbanVibe — Wear Your Vibe.

09. Creative Direction

  • Relatable college-life settings
  • Natural expressions and youthful energy
  • Clear focus on the T-shirts
  • Simple, memorable campaign messaging

10. Why This Idea Works

The idea connects fashion with self-expression. The headline is short and memorable, and the message gives the audience a reason to relate to the brand.

11. What I Learned

This practice project helped me explore audience selection, human insight, brand messaging, headline writing and video-ad concepts.

12. Next Steps

  • Design a finished campaign poster.
  • Refine the video-ad dialogue.
  • Create a scene-by-scene storyboard.
  • Test headline options with potential viewers.

Project disclosure: UrbanVibe is an independent practice concept, not a commissioned client campaign. No real campaign results are claimed.
