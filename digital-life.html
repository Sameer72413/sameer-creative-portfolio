Independent advertising concept · 01

Some Dreams
Need Wi-Fi.

One grandmother wants to see her daughter. One young student wants to build his future. One unreliable connection stands in their way.

More than a connection. A human need.

01 / The big idea

One connection. Two generations.

The internet is more than a screen or a signal. It helps families stay close, students keep learning, and people work towards the future they imagine.

The human insight

Different generations use the internet for different reasons, but both need a reliable connection to what matters to them.

The target audience

Families, students, and people who depend on internet access for communication, education, and creativity.

02 / The advertisement

The story, scene by scene

A first-draft story outline. Dialogue and production details can be developed in the next stage.

01

THE PROBLEM

Two lives, one weak connection

A grandmother video-calls her daughter, but the call keeps freezing. In the same house, her grandson is learning to create a virtual world on his laptop. His online lesson stops because of the weak connection.

The grandmother wants to stay close to her daughter. The boy wants to learn and build his future.

02

THE REALISATION

The father notices

Their father sees how the network problem is affecting both his mother's conversations and his son's learning. He realises that the family needs a more reliable service.

03

THE SOLUTION

A better way to connect

The father compares available internet services and decides to switch to one that better suits the family's needs. The family gets connected again.

04

THE EMOTIONAL PAYOFF

Closer, even from far away

The grandmother, father, and grandson sit together, talking and laughing while the grandmother's daughter joins them through a clear video call. The boy can return to learning and creating his virtual world.

The family enjoys the simple happiness of being connected.

THE CAMPAIGN MESSAGE

Wi-Fi. More Than a Connection. A Human Need.

03 / Creative direction

What this concept stands for

Emotion

The joy of seeing someone you love, even when they are far away.

Ambition

The opportunity to learn, practise, and create something for the future.
